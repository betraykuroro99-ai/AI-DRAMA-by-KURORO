# AI Drama Ending and Delivery

Use when finishing or revising a Kuro Dorama episode. The standing user rule is: hook, story progression, cliffhanger, then ABANGAN with a dramatic fade.

Preserve the cliffhanger and every final dialogue syllable. Fade the final picture to black over approximately 0.6–0.9 seconds, then show ABANGAN clearly on black for an approximately three-second ending card, with a restrained fade in/out. Adjust the picture fade if it would hide a necessary reveal. Do not fade or truncate spoken audio. Keep the title centered within portrait safe margins and use consistent series typography. Do not add future plot spoilers, new dialogue or a paid generation just for the card.

The ending card does not count toward the minimum story duration. Keep natural story pacing and retain the approved portrait resolution, captions and moving translucent Kuro Dorama logo during the episode. A clean ending card may omit the moving logo. Add no abrupt music cut; any new music or sound must be authorized and appropriate for reuse.

Verify output duration, codecs, dimensions, frame rate, final spoken line, fade timing, exact ABANGAN spelling, black end frame, captions, source dialogue order and unchanged story speed. Inspect final card frames and the preceding cliffhanger. Do not claim bit-identical audio if the export reencoded it; distinguish timing preservation from payload identity.

Keep the earlier revision. Publish fresh versioned download filenames to avoid stale caches. Update the complete pack, README, manifest hashes, budget and project status. Test ZIP integrity and download availability. Never include keys, private provider responses, unreleased scripts or surprise notes. Do not post to Facebook unless separately instructed.
