# AI Drama Batch Review

Standing user-requested workflow, committed after the user liked the ten-second Wan sample and explicitly requested the first approximately thirty-second batch. Do not treat that approval as acceptance of any unseen footage.

Work in approximately 30-second editorial batches. Respect the selected model's native limits: MiniMax H3 Max has a 15-second maximum, so a review batch combines shorter clips. Use actual measured dialogue and meaningful action; never stretch speech, freeze frames or loop footage to hit exactly 30 seconds.

Before paid generation, establish the episode spending cap and the concrete batch script, references, voices, prompts and quote. A batch review gate supplements the budget and existing production gates; it does not authorize unapproved spending or automatically approve individual plans.

Generate only the current batch. Track all task IDs and costs; retrieve pending submissions rather than generating duplicates. Assemble a viewable review clip with intended source audio, natural pauses, captions and branding. Inspect faces, hand anatomy, lip movement, exact dialogue, continuity and important actions. Provide the actual clip, actual reserved cost and any known limitations. Do not claim a plan or contact sheet alone is a finished review video.

Stop before generating the next batch. Wait for explicit user approval or revisions. Never infer approval from elapsed time, silence or a status question. Once approved, record the user's exact response, batch ID, artifact hash, actual end states and remaining budget. Carry those states into the next batch. Prepare only necessary repairs when the user requests changes; stay inside the approved cap and preserve older takes. Do not silently commit a rejected take into the final episode.

Complete the episode with cliffhanger and ABANGAN, then recheck transitions across batch boundaries, source dialogue order, audio pacing, minimum story length and final exports. Batch approvals do not replace a final continuity/quality check. Keep future scripts and reveal notes out of review packs unless requested.
