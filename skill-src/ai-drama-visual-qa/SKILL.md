# AI Drama Visual QA

Use after each generated clip and again on the assembled episode. Review actual video at normal speed where playback is available, and inspect enlarged frames around dialogue, head turns, hand contact and cuts. If only sampled frames or automated checks are available, state that limitation; do not claim complete playback or listening.

## Required checks

- Face identity, proportions, eyes, teeth, hairline and jaw remain stable through motion; no melting, flicker, morphing or identity swaps.
- Speaking mouths have restrained articulation, no excessive jaw/cheek movement; listeners do not visibly speak another character's lines. Check alignment against the approved audio where visible speech is used.
- Two anatomically distinct hands; correct thumb placement, fingers, wrists and arm ownership. Injury and prop sides remain consistent. Check the entire action, not just one good frame.
- Clothing, hair, props, door states, lighting, positions and screen direction match accepted continuity. Record actual ending states.
- Every approved dialogue line occurs once, in order, at natural speed; no generated extra voices or unwanted text. Keep consistent approved voices.
- Hook, consequential scene progression and cliffhanger remain comprehensible after edits. No repeated footage or slow padding. ABANGAN follows the cliffhanger and does not replace story duration.

## Repairs

Reject visible face distortion, duplicated hands or severe mouth overacting in delivery footage. First try a same-time crop, a valid action insert or a motivated listener shot without obscuring necessary story information. Otherwise regenerate the affected take only within existing authorization and budget; retain all prior takes and ledger entries. Never count failed attempts as free. Do not submit an extra paid take beyond the user's cap.

Recheck replacement frames, transitions and the full affected interval. Compare export audio/timing to the source baseline. Document timecodes, evidence, chosen repair, residual limitations and additional cost. Automated detection is supporting evidence, not a substitute for visual judgment. Do not claim that prompts guarantee artifact-free generation.
