# Beauty & Bagwis — Episode 9: Ang Ikatlong Kampana

Working cut: approximately 2:10–2:25. Continuous from Episode 8, same morning, inside and directly outside the bakery.

## Hook · 0:00–0:15

Open on the split already in the awning post. The charcoal braid flashes past it into the entrance. A bread tray flips and rolls across the floor. Its crash cuts straight into the sound of Beauty's voice from outside.

VOICE OUTSIDE: “Pa! Dito ako!”

Tomas turns toward the voice. Beauty steps into his sightline from behind the counter.

BEAUTY: “Pa, nandito ako. Tumingin ka sa akin.”

## The fight · 0:15–0:50

The rolling tray passes between Tomas and Bagwis. In its reflection, the braid doubles back along the jamb. Bagwis sees it and shoves the bread rack sideways. The strike lands on the rack in the same shot. The rack skids toward Ning.

MOY: “Ning, dito! Sa likod ng counter!”

Ning pulls Moy down with her just before a tray passes overhead. Sabel moves them farther inside.

BAGWIS: “Kapag narinig mo ulit si Beauty sa labas, dito ka tumingin.”

The lash curls around the rim of the rack and pulls. Bagwis strains to hold the rack away from the others. Tomas starts toward the doorway, catches Beauty's eye, and swings the interior door inward. The braid releases the rack and snaps out through the narrowing gap. The door shuts. Three quick angles cover the pull, Beauty and Tomas making eye contact, and the withdrawal. Stay in real time with overlapping motion through the cuts.

## What it left · 0:50–1:40

Silence for a breath. Bread stops rolling. Moy reaches for Ning's hand under the counter and she takes it. Beauty sees a brass tag on the floor where the braid released the rack. It has three small bell marks. Use an insert to show the symbols clearly.

BEAUTY: “May nalaglag sa sahig. Pa, kilala mo ’to?”

TOMAS: “Tatlong kampana. Ganiyan ang marka ng mga susi sa lumang bantayan.”

SABEL: “Doon dinala si Andres, sabi ng nanay mo. May narinig siyang kampana bago siya nakatakbo.”

BEAUTY: “Alam mo kung saan ’yon, Pa?”

TOMAS: “Oo. Doon ako naghintay sa kanya kinabukasan. Siya mismo ang nagbukas ng pinto para sa akin.”

Bagwis studies Tomas. Let the silence after that line register.

BAGWIS: “Akala mo siya ’yon. Gaya ng akala mo kanina na si Beauty ang tumatawag.”

TOMAS: “Oo. Kung hindi ka nagsalita kanina, anak, lumabas ako.”

## The choice · 1:40–2:15

Tomas looks at the door. He puts his hand down before touching it.

TOMAS: “Babalikan ko ’yung bantayan. Baka may naiwan pa roon.”

BEAUTY: “Sasama ako. Kung may makikita tayo tungkol kay Mama, gusto kong makita mismo.”

BAGWIS: “Sasama rin ako. Kung may marinig tayo, siguraduhin muna nating nakikita natin ang nagsasalita.”

A bell rings from the direction of the hill. Another answers. Tomas looks past the bakery window toward the old road.

SABEL: “Noong gabing ’yon, dalawa lang ang narinig ni Elena.”

A third bell rings. The front door handle moves from the outside.

VOICE OUTSIDE, ANDRES: “Tomas. Ako ang nagbukas noon. Ikaw naman ngayon.”

Hold on Tomas recognizing the voice. Fade to ABANGAN.
