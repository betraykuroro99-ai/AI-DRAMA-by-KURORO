# The Watchmaker — opening sequence

On a rainy night, elderly watchmaker Chen works beneath a warm desk lamp. His granddaughter Tang asks him to close the shop. A delivery rider arrives, removes his helmet and places a wrapped pocket watch on the counter.

Chen turns it over. Beneath the grime, the engraving reads "Keep Faith." His loupe falls onto the green felt.

"Where did you get this watch?" he asks.

"My father left it. He died last month. I found it wrapped in a handkerchief, inside his drawer."

The rider says his father learned the trade down south but never talked about it.

"What was his name?"

"Gu Changming."

Chen lowers his head. Tang puts down her pencil and watches him. Outside, the rain keeps falling.
